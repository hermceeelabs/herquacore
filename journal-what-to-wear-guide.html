Early Signs of Hidden Pipe Leaks & How to Protect Your Building

Spotting wall dampness, low water pressure, and underground pipe seepage before structural damage occurs.

1. Wall Dampness, Peeling Paint & Efflorescence

Unexplained moisture patches on internal or external walls, blistering paint, or damp skirting boards are the earliest indicators of a pinhole leak in a concealed water supply line behind bathroom or kitchen tiles.

2. Rapid Overhead Tank Depletion & Pump Cycling

If your overhead storage tank empties overnight when taps are shut, or your pressure booster pump switches on repeatedly without water usage, an underground supply pipe or concealed WC cistern valve is losing water.

3. Non-Destructive Pressure Isolation & Repiping

By isolating individual pipe branches and pressure-testing each line, our technicians pinpoint the exact leaking joint without tearing down entire walls—replacing faulty elbows or pipes with heat-fused PPR fittings.

Summary

Key Takeaways

  • Address wall dampness early before moisture weakens plaster and concrete.
  • Check WC flush valves and underground unions if your tank drains unexpectedly.
  • Always pressure-test newly installed or repaired pipes before tiling over walls.
