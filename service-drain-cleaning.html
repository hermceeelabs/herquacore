Scope of Work

Kitchen & Bathroom Drain Unblocking

Removal of grease buildup, soap residue, hair, and sediment blockages from kitchen sinks, floor drains, and shower traps.

Main Sewer & Inspection Chamber Clearing

Deep rodding, flushing, and desilting of external inspection chambers, manholes, and underground waste pipes.

Preventive Drainage Maintenance

Routine line cleaning and trap servicing to eliminate slow drains, foul odors, and wastewater backflow.

How We Work

Our 3-Step Delivery Process

01

Blockage Inspection

We trace the exact point of obstruction in your internal drain traps or external inspection chambers.

02

Mechanical Clearing & Flushing

Blocked lines are thoroughly cleared and flushed to remove hardened debris, grease, and silt.

03

Flow Verification

We test all connected sinks, floor drains, and gullies to confirm fast, odor-free drainage.
