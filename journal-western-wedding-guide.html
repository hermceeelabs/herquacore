Complete Guide to Borehole Drilling & Geophysical Survey

How pre-drilling aquifer surveys, proper PVC casing, and submersible pump sizing guarantee year-round clean water.

1. Pre-Drilling Geophysical Survey

Before mobilizing a drilling rig on your property in Abuja or anywhere across Nigeria, conducting a vertical electrical sounding (VES) geophysical survey maps out underground rock fractures and water-bearing aquifers. This prevents dry holes and identifies the exact depth required for sustainable year-round water yield.

2. Rotary & DTH Drilling, Casing & Gravel Packing

Using heavy-duty drilling rigs suited for both basement complex rock and sedimentary formations, the borehole is drilled to the target aquifer depth. High-pressure uPVC casings and screen pipes are installed, followed by washed river gravel packing to naturally filter out silt and sediment.

3. Well Flushing & Submersible Pump Commissioning

After casing, high-pressure air flushing clears all drilling mud until crystal-clear water flows continuously. Finally, a properly sized stainless-steel submersible pump, riser pipes, and automatic float control are installed to deliver water directly into your overhead tank.

Summary

Key Takeaways

  • Always run a geophysical survey before drilling to pinpoint aquifer depth.
  • Use pressure-rated uPVC casings and clean filter gravel to prevent well collapse or muddy water.
  • Match submersible pump capacity to borehole yield and overhead tank height.
